Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, fees, verification, and class access.

How do I enroll in OAV Mantra Classes?
Click on the "Enroll Now" button anywhere on the page, fill in your student details (Name, 10-digit Mobile, Email, Class, and City/School), submit the form, and complete the ₹500 registration fee via UPI. You will receive a unique Enrollment ID.
What is the registration fee and payment method?
The registration fee is a one-time payment of ₹500. You can pay seamlessly using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) by scanning our official QR code or using Quick UPI intent.
How soon after payment will my class study room unlock?
Once you enter your UPI transaction reference ID, your account is queued for administrator verification. As soon as the administrator verifies your payment (typically within 1-2 hours), your study room and course resources unlock immediately.
Can students from normal state schools (non-OAV) enroll?
Yes, absolutely! During enrollment, choose "Normal" under School Type. Our curriculum covers the standard Odisha State and CBSE syllabus applicable to all school students.
